How an order is built

By the unit, the inner or the master case.

Every line carries its own pack structure. You choose the level you buy at, mix levels inside one purchase order, and reorder the whole thing next month without rebuilding it.

Pack structurePer line

A line like Foger 30K carries 82 flavours across two styles — 157 orderable SKUs. You can take a single flavour by the inner, or the whole display by the master case. Quantities and pricing for each level appear on the line inside your account.

Unit Single SKU — one flavour, one style, one colour. The smallest thing you can put on an order. Qty in account
Inner Shelf pack — the sealed inner a shop puts straight on the counter without breaking it down. Qty in account
Master Master case — full factory case, mixed or single flavour depending on the line. Palletised for delivery. Qty in account
